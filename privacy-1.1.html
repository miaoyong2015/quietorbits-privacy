太阳系3D · 静谧星轨 — 隐私政策

适用于含 Nova 功能的 1.1 版。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日。

太阳系浏览、天体资料、系统朗读、生日与体重计算等核心功能都在设备上运行，不需要账号。Pro 童声和成人声资源首次使用时可能需要从 App Store 下载，下载后可离线播放。我们没有广告、统计或跨应用追踪。

可选的 Nova 问答

  • 只有你主动打开 Nova 并同意提示后，应用才会联网发送你输入的问题、最多三轮对话上下文、所选语言与当前天体。请不要输入姓名、学校、地址或其他个人信息。
  • 应用会发送一个随机生成的安装标识，用于免费次数和使用限额。该标识保存在设备钥匙串中，重新安装后可能继续存在。服务器使用其哈希值记录次数，不用它投放广告或追踪其他应用。
  • 问题由我们在 Cloudflare 上运行的 AI 服务处理。我们的应用数据库不保存原始问题或对话上下文；为避免重复生成，会缓存回答及后续问题建议，最长 30 天。限额记录最长保留约 400 天，IP 地址的哈希限额记录约 2 天。
  • 选择 Nova 的在线朗读时，回答文字会发送至 Cloudflare 托管的 MeloTTS 模型以生成音频。当前在线成人配音支持中、英、日、韩、法、西班牙语，其余语言或音色使用系统朗读。生成的音频会在我们的服务器缓存最长 30 天；服务不可用时使用设备上的系统语音。语音输入由设备系统的语音识别服务处理，具体处理方式取决于设备设置和系统服务。
  • 如果使用 Pro，我们会把 Apple 签名的购买凭证发送给服务器验证权限，并保留购买交易编号的哈希值作为用量限制记录，约两天后由每日清理任务删除。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到支付卡资料。

可以在「设置 → Nova 隐私 → 撤回 Nova 使用同意」撤回同意。再次同意前不会发送新的 Nova 问题；已经发送的请求及已保留的服务器记录按上述期限处理，不会因撤回而自动立即删除。太阳系、重力实验和本地资料仍可使用。

Cloudflare 的 Workers AI 数据使用说明表示，未经明确同意，不会使用客户输入和输出训练模型或改进其服务。我们的回答、音频和限额记录仍按上述期限存储，这不等于所有服务都零留存。

我的星球护照

1.1.0 测试版 build 3 起恢复简单的星球盖章和星星记录，保存在此设备上。不提供添加照片或在线 AI 图像生成功能；照片制作留待后续版本。

早期 build 2 测试版曾提供可选的本地照片合成：仅取得用户通过系统选择器主动选定的照片，缩小并重新编码，不保留 EXIF/位置元数据，不上传。该版保留的照片/成品未在本次更新中自动删除，仍只在本设备，卸载应用会移除这些应用数据。

其他本地数据

  • 你输入的生日和体重只保存在设备上，用于计算其他星球上的年龄和体重；卸载应用会删除这些本地数据。
  • 当你选择分享或保存截图时，图片由系统分享面板处理；应用不浏览你的相册。

Privacy Policy — Solar System 3D: Quiet Orbits

Applies to version 1.1 with Nova. Updated October 7, 2026.

Core features, including the solar system, educational content, system narration, and age and weight calculations, run on your device without an account. Pro child and adult voice packs may need an initial download from the App Store; once downloaded, they can play offline. We use no ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Optional Ask Nova

  • Only after you open Nova and accept its notice does the app send your question, up to three previous exchanges, your selected language, and the current celestial body online. Please do not enter names, schools, addresses, or other personal information.
  • The app sends a random installation identifier to manage free trials and usage limits. It is kept in the device Keychain and may survive reinstalling the app. Our server stores a hash of it for quota records. We do not use it for advertising or tracking across apps.
  • Our AI service runs on Cloudflare. Our application database does not store raw questions or conversation history. To avoid regenerating common answers, it caches answers and suggested follow-up questions for up to 30 days. Quota records are kept for up to approximately 400 days; hashed IP quota records for approximately two days.
  • If you choose Nova's online narration, answer text is sent to the MeloTTS model hosted by Cloudflare to generate audio. Online adult narration currently supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French and Spanish; other languages or voice styles use system narration. The generated audio is cached on our server for up to 30 days. If that service fails, the app uses the device's system voice. Voice input is handled by the device's speech recognition service; its processing depends on your device settings and system service.
  • For Pro, we send an Apple-signed purchase transaction to our server to verify access. We retain a hash of its transaction identifier for usage limits; a daily cleanup removes it after approximately two days. Apple handles payment; we do not receive payment-card details.

You can withdraw consent in Settings → Nova privacy → Withdraw Nova consent. No new Nova questions are sent until you agree again. Requests already sent and existing server records are handled under the retention periods above; withdrawal does not immediately delete them. The solar system, gravity experiments and local educational content remain available.

Cloudflare's Workers AI data usage policy states that it does not use customer inputs or outputs to train models or improve its services without explicit consent. Our answer, audio and quota records are still stored for the periods above; this is not a promise of zero retention across all services.

My Space Passport

Starting with 1.1.0 beta build 3, the passport contains simple planet stamps and star counts stored on your device. It does not offer adding photos or online AI image generation. Photo creation is deferred to a future version.

Earlier beta build 2 offered optional on-device photo composition. It received only the photo you selected with the system picker, resized and re-encoded it without EXIF/location metadata, and did not upload it. This update does not automatically delete photo copies or generated passports kept by that earlier beta; they remain on this device and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Other local data

  • Birthday and weight entered for planetary comparisons stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • When you choose to share or save a screenshot, the system share sheet handles the image. The app does not browse your photo library.

For information about Nova data or a deletion request, contact us below. Because there are no accounts, we may be unable to associate anonymous cached content with a particular person.
