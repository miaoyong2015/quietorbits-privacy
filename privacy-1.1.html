太阳系3D · 静谧星轨 — 隐私政策

适用于含 Nova 功能的 1.1 版。更新日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日。

太阳系浏览、天体资料、系统朗读、生日与体重计算等核心功能都在设备上运行，不需要账号。Pro 童声和成人声资源首次使用时可能需要从 App Store 下载，下载后可离线播放。我们没有广告、统计或跨应用追踪。

可选的 Nova 问答

  • 只有你主动打开 Nova 并同意提示后，应用才会联网发送你输入的问题、最多三轮对话上下文、所选语言与当前天体。请不要输入姓名、学校、地址或其他个人信息。
  • 应用会发送一个随机生成的安装标识，用于免费次数和使用限额。该标识保存在设备钥匙串中，重新安装后可能继续存在。服务器使用其哈希值记录次数，不用它投放广告或追踪其他应用。
  • 问题由我们在 Cloudflare 上运行的 AI 服务处理。我们的应用数据库不保存原始问题或对话上下文；为避免重复生成，会缓存回答及后续问题建议，最长 30 天。限额记录最长保留约 400 天，IP 地址的哈希限额记录约 2 天。
  • 选择 Nova 的在线朗读时，回答文字会发送至火山引擎/豆包语音服务以生成音频。生成的音频会在我们的服务器缓存最长 30 天；服务不可用时使用设备上的系统语音。语音输入由设备系统的语音识别服务处理，具体处理方式取决于设备设置和系统服务。
  • 如果使用 Pro，我们会把 Apple 签名的购买凭证发送给服务器验证权限，并保留购买交易编号的哈希值作为用量限制记录，约两天后由每日清理任务删除。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到支付卡资料。

会员太空护照

当前测试版仅提供本地照片合成，在线 AI 图像生成尚未开放。会员可使用系统照片选择器，选择一张照片制作太空护照。我们只接收你主动选定的照片，不浏览相册；照片不会上传。

其他本地数据

会员可使用系统照片选择器，将选定照片制作成太空护照。照片只在此设备缩小、重新编码并合成，不保留 EXIF/位置元数据；保留一份选定照片和一份已生成成品，并排除系统备份，可替换或移除，不上传。生成护照的分享由系统分享面板处理。

  • 你输入的生日和体重只保存在设备上，用于计算其他星球上的年龄和体重；卸载应用会删除这些本地数据。
  • 当你选择分享或保存截图时，图片由系统分享面板处理；应用不浏览你的相册；系统照片选择器仅提供你主动选定的照片。

如需了解或请求删除与 Nova 有关的服务器数据，请通过下方邮箱联系。由于不设账号，我们可能无法把匿名缓存与某个人对应。

Privacy Policy — Solar System 3D: Quiet Orbits

Applies to version 1.1 with Nova. Updated October 5, 2026.

Core features, including the solar system, educational content, system narration, and age and weight calculations, run on your device without an account. Pro child and adult voice packs may need an initial download from the App Store; once downloaded, they can play offline. We use no ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Optional Ask Nova

  • Only after you open Nova and accept its notice does the app send your question, up to three previous exchanges, your selected language, and the current celestial body online. Please do not enter names, schools, addresses, or other personal information.
  • The app sends a random installation identifier to manage free trials and usage limits. It is kept in the device Keychain and may survive reinstalling the app. Our server stores a hash of it for quota records. We do not use it for advertising or tracking across apps.
  • Our AI service runs on Cloudflare. Our application database does not store raw questions or conversation history. To avoid regenerating common answers, it caches answers and suggested follow-up questions for up to 30 days. Quota records are kept for up to approximately 400 days; hashed IP quota records for approximately two days.
  • If you choose Nova's online narration, answer text is sent to Volcengine/Doubao to generate audio. The generated audio is cached on our server for up to 30 days. If that service fails, the app uses the device's system voice. Voice input is handled by the device's speech recognition service; its processing depends on your device settings and system service.
  • For Pro, we send an Apple-signed purchase transaction to our server to verify access. We retain a hash of its transaction identifier for usage limits; a daily cleanup removes it after approximately two days. Apple handles payment; we do not receive payment-card details.

Pro space passport

The current beta offers on-device photo composition only. Online AI image generation is not available. The system photo picker gives the app only the photo you choose; the app does not browse your library or upload that photo.

Other local data

  • Birthday and weight entered for planetary comparisons stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • When you choose to share or save a screenshot, the system share sheet handles the image. The app does not browse your photo library. For an optional Pro space-passport photo, the system photo picker provides only the image you select. The app resizes and re-encodes it on this device without EXIF/location metadata, keeps one selected-photo copy and one generated passport outside system backups, and lets you replace or remove it. The photo is not uploaded. Sharing a generated passport uses the system share sheet.

For information about Nova data or a deletion request, contact us below. Because there are no accounts, we may be unable to associate anonymous cached content with a particular person.
