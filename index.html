太阳系3D · 静谧星轨 — 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日

静谧星轨（以下简称“本应用”）完全在你的设备上离线运行。

  • 不收集任何个人信息。本应用不包含账号、广告、统计或第三方追踪 SDK，不联网上传任何数据。
  • 你在应用内填写的生日和体重仅用于计算“你在其他星球上的年龄和体重”，只保存在本机，卸载应用即删除。
  • 截图保存到相册时，系统会请求“添加到相册”的权限；本应用只写入你主动保存的图片，不读取你的相册。
  • 一次性购买由 Apple App Store 处理，我们无法获取你的支付信息。
  • 本应用适合儿童使用，不向儿童收集任何信息。

Privacy Policy — Solar System 3D: Quiet Orbits

Updated October 3, 2026

  • We collect no personal data. The app works fully offline, with no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking.
  • The birthday and weight you may enter are used only to show your age and weight on other worlds. They stay on your device and are deleted with the app.
  • Saving a screenshot asks for permission to add to your photo library; the app only writes images you choose to save and never reads your library.
  • The one-time purchase is handled by Apple; we never see payment details.
  • The app is suitable for children and collects no information from them.
